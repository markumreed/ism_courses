# Peer review of decision records (case due weeks)

In the meeting of each case's due week (Weeks 8, 10, 12, and 14), you trade **draft decision records** with the student next to you and review each other's reasoning before the Sunday deadline. It takes about 12 minutes per person. If no one is next to you, join the nearest pair as a group of three, or post in the class Q&A and a TA will match you.

## What you share, and what you don't

- **Share:** your draft decision record (section 8 of your notebook, about 200–300 words) and **one** chart from section 4, on your screen.
- **Don't share:** your notebook, code, cleaned data, or numbers beyond what that chart and record show.
- You may change your own work because of what you heard. You may not copy your reviewer's text, code, or numbers. Cases are individual work.

## How it runs

1. **Read (2 minutes).** The reviewer reads the record and looks at the chart. The author says nothing.
2. **Write (5 minutes).** The reviewer answers the four questions below in writing, on paper or in a note on their own device, and shows the answers to the author.
3. **Talk (3 minutes).** The author asks about anything unclear. The author does not defend the work; the goal is to hear how a reader understood it.
4. **Decide (2 minutes).** The author writes one change they will make before submitting.

Then switch roles.

## The four questions

1. **Frame the question.** In one sentence, what does this record recommend? If you can't say, write what confused you.
2. **Choose meaningful comparisons.** What number supports the recommendation? Is the comparison fair: the same denominator, the same period, and the right rows?
3. **Check the evidence.** Name one data problem the author handled, and one item from the verification checklist you couldn't see evidence for.
4. **Explain the decision.** What would a skeptical manager ask first?
