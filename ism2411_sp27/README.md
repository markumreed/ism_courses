# ISM 2411 Introduction to Python for Business — Spring 2027

Course materials for the Spring 2027 redesign of ISM 2411. All programming happens in **Google Colab**, in Jupyter notebooks. Students save their work from Colab into their own public GitHub repository, `ism2411`, and submit GitHub URLs in Canvas. All companies, people, vendors, and data here are fictional, generated for teaching.

## How the course works

- **One meeting a week.** Meetings in Weeks 3–14 open with a closed-book check on the previous week's material: 12 checks, on Weeks 2–13. Week 14's material is assessed in the Finance case and the portfolio.
- **Due dates:** everything is due Sunday at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time of its week. The portfolio is the one exception: Monday, May 3.
- **Late work:** a 25% grade reduction (25% of the possible points) for each calendar day late, subject to approved accommodations.
- **In class:** polls in Poll Everywhere, answered while signed in with your USF account, record attendance and count toward participation (10% of the grade).
- **Attendance:** missing more than four class meetings, for any reason, including illness, results in automatic failure of the course. Absences are logged from your Poll Everywhere sign-in; you do not need to report or explain them, and there are no exceptions. If you expect to miss four or more meetings, drop the course.
- **Labs** carry no points and are marked Complete or Incomplete. Each check includes one question that is a variant of an exercise in that week's lab.
- **Data:** every notebook opens in Colab straight from this repository and loads its data from here, so there is nothing to upload.

New to Colab and GitHub? Start with the [technical guide](cases/templates/TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md).
