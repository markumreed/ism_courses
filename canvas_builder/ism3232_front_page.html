Canvas only tracks Modules, Assignments, and grades. All readings, lecture videos, lab instructions, self-check quizzes, and cheat sheets live on the course website — that's your daily starting point, not this Canvas course.
Coming from ISM 2411?
Good — this course picks up where ISM 2411 left off. Module 1 assumes you're comfortable with functions, loops, and dictionaries and moves straight into a professional developer workflow: the terminal, virtual environments, and Git.

How each week works
1
 Read & watch. Work through that week's reading and lecture video on the course website.
2
 Attend lab. Come to the in-person lab (starting Week 2) and work through that week's material hands-on — laptop required.
3
 Submit by Sunday, 11:59 PM. Turn in that week's assignment as a GitHub URL — not a file upload — here in Canvas.
